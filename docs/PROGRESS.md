## 2026-11-01: traffic, multi-track, station signals, train types, weather, all levels

**What was built**
- **Signalling:**
  - automatic block signalling (RED / Y / YY / G from occupancy plus 180 m overlap);
  - every station has a distant, home, starter and advanced starter, plus a loop starter where there is a loop;
  - a dispatcher sets home routes (main or loop), holds starters until dwell is complete, and grants overtakes by priority.
- **Lines:** double line (UP/DN) and station loops, modelled as lateral offsets on one chainage, so the physics stays one-dimensional.
- **AI traffic:** deterministic kinematic AI trains that obey signals and booked stops and never pass red (asserted by a test).
- **Train types:** Superfast (22 LHB), Mail Express (18), Passenger (diesel, 12), Suburban EMU (12-car), Goods (58 wagons, about 4,700 t). Each has its own livery and model.
- **Levels:** five, all unlocked, on routes generated by `tools/build-routes.ts`.
- **Weather:**
  - clear and summer haze;
  - monsoon rain: wet adhesion and braking, rain streaks;
  - winter fog: signal colour hidden beyond about 250 m, 75 km/h game-rule cap;
  - night: stars, headlight, platform lamps.
- **Notifications:** "signal cleared, you may start", "routed into the loop", "train held for you", "train passing", with a chime. The timetable line shows due and late times.
- **Pickup:** the gameplay pickup boost is raised to 1.7 on all locos.
- **Ground:** the ground now follows the track's gradients, so the line is never buried.

**How it was verified**
- `npm test`: 74 tests pass, including:
  - interlocking aspect chains;
  - the starter clearing after dwell;
  - AI never passing red;
  - the Mail loop overtake;
  - longer braking in rain;
  - fog visibility;
  - determinism with traffic;
  - every level driven to the end by a rule-abiding autopilot.
- `npm run test:e2e`: 5 pass, including every level and weather loaded from the menu with no console errors.
- `npm run perf:sim`: at most about 20 µs per sim step with traffic.
- Browser screenshots:
  - Mail loop hold, with the Superfast passing on the main line;
  - Intercity passenger held in the Rampur loop;
  - fog cab view with "Not in sight yet";
  - night;
  - rain.
- Intercity chase view: 121 draw calls and 1.2 M triangles.

**What remains**
- Turnouts are smooth S-curves, not modelled switch blades.
- AI trains use a kinematic driver, not the air-brake model.
- Diesel and EMU figures are UNVERIFIED approximations.
- Triangle count is dominated by instanced sleepers and could be reduced with sleeper LOD.

## 2026-11-03: accidents — collisions, derailments, buffer stops

**What was built**
- **Accidents on/off** (menu, default on). With accidents on, a SPAD sounds an alarm and the train runs on: stopping gives a near-miss report; hitting something crashes. Off keeps the instant SPAD fail.
- **Mishaps:**
  - rear-end and head-on collisions;
  - side collisions at points, e.g. leaving the Sitapur loop against the red loop starter while the Superfast passes;
  - derailment above 1.6× the points' speed limit or above 2.5 m/s² unbalanced lateral acceleration on curves (game rules, approximate);
  - buffer-stop overrun at the new Suburban terminus (Sangam); touching them at 8 km/h or less is only a bump.
- **Crash simulation** (`src/sim/crash/`, deterministic):
  - every vehicle is a rigid body, with couplers that buckle into a zig-zag, ends that crumple, override, tipping, bogies torn off and up to 160 debris pieces;
  - all signals go to danger after an accident, and AI drivers brake (1.5 s reaction) when they sight a train that has run a red signal.
- **Presentation:**
  - slow-motion accident camera orbiting the impact; wreck poses, scorch tint, debris, sparks, dust and smoke, camera shake;
  - synthesized impact, crunch and screech sounds and a SPAD alarm;
  - accident inquiry card (cause, speeds, timeline, damage); R replays from a new angle with a 3 s approach pre-roll; Enter restarts.
- Occupancy now counts a train foul of an adjacent line, so signals protect turnouts. The goods loop was lengthened so its 640 m rake clears the main line.

**How it was verified**
- `npm test`: crash tests cover:
  - the SPAD flow, on and off;
  - rear-end, side and buffer collisions;
  - derailment at 55 km/h but not at 36 km/h on 30 km/h points;
  - the near miss where the AI brakes in time;
  - all signals at danger after an accident;
  - finite poses, energy draining and settling within 15 s;
  - identical replays.
- `npm run test:e2e`: a scripted rear-end crash shows the accident camera, the inquiry card, the replay and the restart, with no console errors. Screenshots were taken of the rear-end wreck, the side collision, the derailment and the replay.

**What remains**
- Crash physics is a stylised approximation; its thresholds are game rules.
- Crash visuals have not been timed at real FPS on a laptop GPU (headless software rendering runs at about 2 fps).
- There is no head-on scenario yet: no level puts the player against opposing traffic on the same line.

## 2026-11-03: living world vertical slice (addendum wave 1 + slice)

**What was built**
- **Research (wave 1, three parallel researchers):**
  - `docs/research/05-scene-zones.md`: corridor sequences, ghat figures, crops, spans and open data;
  - `docs/research/07-yards.md`: turnout speeds, route indicators and interlocking;
  - `docs/IMMERSION_IDEAS.md`: 19 ranked ideas, station life, and the ACP procedure under s.141.
  - Seven new subagents are defined in `.claude/agents/`.
- **Scene zones:** zones are data (`content/zones/zones.json`, 12 biomes), and every route has a scene script. Weights blend over 400 m, driving terrain relief, vegetation, buildings, colour grading and ambient sound. Dressing is precomputed per 500 m chunk and streamed into 14 instanced meshes around the camera.
- **Pilot route "Ghat Express"** (fictional, inspired by Western Ghats crossings):
  - city junction, suburbs, industrial belt and farmland with a level crossing;
  - a river girder bridge;
  - forest, then the ghat with two tunnels and a masonry viaduct;
  - a hill-station terminus with buffer stops.
  - The ghat climb of 1 in 60 is a design value, gentler than the cited 1 in 40 of the historic Bhor Ghat.
- **Junction yard (Nagarpur Jn):**
  - two numbered platform lines on crossover ladders plus the main line;
  - 30 km/h turnouts, per the cited 1 in 12 figure;
  - booked platforms set the route;
  - a junction-type route indicator lights on the home signal;
  - conflicting exits never clear together;
  - turnout overspeed is a recorded violation.
- **Station life:**
  - deterministic crowds of about 430 agents (passengers, porters, vendors, staff, police) that queue back from the edge, board, alight, wave, and run for the train without boarding it;
  - stalls, benches, a departures board, a foot-over-bridge and a stray dog at halts.
- **Narration:**
  - Hindi, English and Marathi (draft) arrival and delay announcements through Web Speech, always subtitled;
  - crew radio;
  - a journey log of regions and landmarks;
  - cited "Did you know?" cards.
- **ACP incident:** the chain vents the brake pipe, the train stops, the crew resets the valve after 45 s (compressed from the cited 15–20 min), and a debrief cites s.141.
- **Hooks:**
  - asset files of any type are refused without a licence row;
  - content edits run the scene and yard tests;
  - Stop also runs `perf:world`.

**How it was verified**
- `npm test`: tests cover zone continuity and order, the journey log, yard reachability, the booked route and indicator, no conflicting clears, turnout violations, crowd determinism and queueing, and the ACP flow.
- `npm run perf:world`: every route, zone type and preset is within its dressing budget; the busiest view is about 1,400 instances and 71k triangles at Medium. The crowd sim takes 3.4 µs per step.
- `npm run perf:sim`: within budget. It now has a warm-up run, cached train footprints and 30 Hz signalling.
- e2e: the pilot level shows the region line, a subtitled announcement, the crowd and the ACP debrief, with no console errors.
- Headless screenshots of every zone, the junction, the station crowd, the tunnel (cab), the viaduct and the hill station.

**What remains**
- Further waves: more routes per mode, five more narrated incidents, the top-5 immersion picks, and real OSM/DEM corridors.
- Turnouts are still S-curves without moving switch blades.
- Marathi announcement text needs native review. Speech voices vary by device.
- Real-laptop FPS is unmeasured (the headless renderer is software).
